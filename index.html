Frequently Asked Questions

Is MedCompare affiliated with any pharmacy?

No. We're an independent price comparison tool. We don't own, run, or have a financial relationship with PharmEasy, Truemeds, 1mg, or Apollo Pharmacy — we simply check their publicly listed prices for you.

How do you get these prices — are they accurate?

We fetch prices directly from each pharmacy's own site at the moment you search. Prices can still change quickly, vary by stock or delivery pincode, or differ slightly by the time you check out — always confirm the final price on the pharmacy's page before paying.

Do you sell or store my personal data?

We don't require an account, and we don't collect payment or health information. Your search terms may be logged anonymously to power the "Popular Searches" feature — nothing more.

Why is a pharmacy sometimes missing from my results?

Some pharmacy sites occasionally block automated price checks or don't have the exact medicine listed. When that happens we say so honestly instead of showing a guessed price.

Do you make money from this?

Not currently — there's no advertising or affiliate revenue built in today. If that ever changes, it won't affect how results are ranked; price will always come first.
